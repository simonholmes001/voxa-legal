# voxa-legal Voxa Privacy Policy

Voxa Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Voxa is an AI language tutor app. This policy explains what information Voxa collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Contact

For privacy questions, data export requests, or deletion requests, contact: hello@tinyrobotcompany.io.

Information We Collect

Voxa may collect and process the following information:

  • Sign in with Apple identifiers used to create and secure your account.
  • Language profile information, including target language, native language, level, goals, and daily learning preferences.
  • Learning progress, lesson history, review items, tutor summaries, mistakes, and recommended next practice.
  • Text you enter into practice, ask-anything, and translation features.
  • Voice input and audio used for spoken tutoring, voice questions, and voice translation.
  • Images you choose to submit for image translation.
  • App interaction and diagnostic data, such as session activity, request status, correlation IDs, and operational logs.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Provide language tutoring, translation, review, and practice features.
  • Personalize lessons and learning recommendations.
  • Maintain your learning progress across sessions and devices.
  • Secure accounts and authenticated app sessions.
  • Monitor service reliability, prevent abuse, and manage API usage costs.
  • Support account export and deletion requests.

AI Processing

Voxa uses backend services to process some learning content with OpenAI APIs. This may include text, audio, translations, image-translation content, lesson generation, and tutor feedback. Voxa does not include permanent OpenAI API keys in the iOS app.

Service Providers

Voxa may use the following providers to operate the service:

  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple and App Store/TestFlight services.
  • Microsoft Azure, for backend hosting, storage, monitoring, and security infrastructure.
  • OpenAI, for AI tutoring, translation, speech, image, and lesson-generation features.
  • GitHub, for development, deployment, and release automation.

Permissions

Voxa asks for device permissions only when needed for a feature:

  • Microphone and speech recognition are used for spoken tutoring and voice input.
  • Camera and photo access are used for image translation when you choose an image.
  • Notifications are used for learning reminders if you allow them.

Data Retention

Voxa keeps account and learning data while your account is active so the app can provide progress tracking and personalized tutoring. Operational logs are retained for service reliability and security purposes. We aim to minimize retained diagnostic content and avoid storing raw sensitive learner content in logs.

Account Deletion and Data Export

Voxa provides in-app controls to export your account data and delete your account. Deleting your account removes your learner state, refresh sessions, realtime audit rows, and realtime rate-limit rows associated with your account, unless retention is required for legal or security reasons.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact hello@tinyrobotcompany.io to exercise these rights.

Tracking and Advertising

Voxa does not use your data for third-party advertising or tracking.

International Processing

Voxa and its providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Voxa changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
